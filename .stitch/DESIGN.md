# Neon Night Design System

## Overview
The design system captures the electrifying energy of live stage karaoke. High-contrast neon glows meet deep midnight surfaces and glassmorphism.

## Components Specification
- **Glass Container**: 8% opacity white surface with subtle borders and deep background tone `#131318`.
- **Neon Glows**: Diffused purple (`#bd00ff`) and cyan (`#00eefc`) glows for active items.
- **Score Displays**: Monospaced score metrics (`JetBrains Mono`) paired with huge glowing display numbers (`Anybody`).
